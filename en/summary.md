# Confirm the installation
This step lists all options you selected earlier ->
![](../img/summary.webp)
Review the installation contents here. **If something is not right, you can go back and change it.**

Everything can be changed until you press **Next** and confirm the installation.
::: danger Warning
Carefully check that the disk partitions are correct. This step destroys everything on the selected target partitions. ReSpring ClipsNeko Co., Ltd. cannot be held responsible for resulting data loss.
:::
Once you confirm the installation, the installation steps begin and the disks are formatted.
